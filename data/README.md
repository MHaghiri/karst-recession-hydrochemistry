# Data

Input data are not distributed with this repository. They are available from
the corresponding author (mhaghi2@uic.edu) on reasonable request.

Expected inputs (names as used by the scripts):

- `hydrodynamic.xlsx`   monthly discharge, springs S1-S11 and qanats Q1-Q4
- `hydrochemical.xlsx`  major ions, EC, TDS, pH and temperature, wet and dry seasons
- `Isotope.xlsx`        d18O, d2H, coordinates and elevation (sheets `Sheet1`, `IMWL`)
- `study_area.shp`      study-area boundary (UTM zone 39N)
